Find elements on the current page of the built-in browser by the words they
show, and get their refs for browser_act, without reading the whole page.

Every word of `query` has to appear on the element's line of the outline (its
role, name, href or placeholder), with accents and case ignored. Each match is
listed as its outline line, under the heading it sits beneath, so the answers of
one question come grouped under that question.

Use it when you know what you are looking for and the page is big: the
"Próxima" button, the answers under "Questão 3", the link to a lesson, the
search box. It costs a few hundred characters where a whole outline costs tens
of thousands. Pass `role` to keep only buttons, links, radios and so on.

When nothing to act on matches, the result says whether the words appear in the
page's text, so you know the page has them. To read a whole section, use
browser_snapshot with `within`.
