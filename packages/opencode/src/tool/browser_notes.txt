Keep notes about a website that will save time the next time you use it. They
are shown to you, with the programs saved for the site, the first time you land
on it in a later session.

Write a note when you find out something you would otherwise have to discover
again: where a thing is ("the lessons are under Meus cursos > course > Seção"),
a step the site needs ("after the last question, press Enviar tudo e terminar,
then confirm in the dialog"), or a trap ("the PDF opens in a new tab; read it,
then come back"). One fact per note, short, in words that stay true from one
visit to the next: no refs, and no ids that change.

Notes are about how the site works, never about the user's private data: no
passwords, answers to personal questions, grades or documents.

- `add`: the note to keep, for the site of the page the browser is on (or of
  `url`, when given).
- `remove`: numbers of notes to delete, as they are listed, for notes that
  turned out wrong or out of date.

Returns the site's notes as they are now.
