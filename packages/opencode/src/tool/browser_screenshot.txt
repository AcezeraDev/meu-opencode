Take a screenshot of the current page of the built-in browser and attach it as an
image.

Only useful when you can actually look at images, and when the visual result is
what matters: layout, styling, charts, or checking that a page renders correctly.
For reading content or finding elements to click, browser_snapshot is far cheaper
and more precise.

Pass `ref` or `selector` to capture a single element, or `fullPage` to capture the
entire scrollable page instead of just the viewport.
